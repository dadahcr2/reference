HCR2 10th Anniversary IS LIVE 🎂 - kicking off a whole month of celebrations, new maps, a new game mode and exclusive rewards.

Here’s what is waiting for you in HCR2 right now:  

🔥PLAY NOW:"The Climb"

Our first-ever roguelike event mode is here.
Build your deck, dodge run-ending penalties and see how far you can push through Climb Canyon tracks with a twist.

🛘 EXPLORE: The Great Below

The descent begins. Navigate the floating terrain, surreal anomalies and bottomless pits of our newest adventure track!

🏆 NEW CUP: The Deep Caverns Cup

Ready to race deep below? Test your driving through the dark, treacherous underground in our newest competitive Cup!

💎 NEW: The Loyalty Bit Shop

Treat yourself! Earn Loyalty Bits on in-game purchases and spend them in the all new Loyalty Shop to claim exclusive cosmetics, upgrades and rare goodies.

⚙️ FEED THE MACHINE: Fuse-O-Matic

Hit Legendary Rank? Don't let your duplicate Legendary Parts collect dust. Fuse 10 Legendary parts for 1 random Mythic Part! (Remember: part #10 locks it in!)

📜 THE HUNT BEGINS: Canyon Chronicle

Canyon Chronicle pages are now hidden across the game. Track them down, uncover the untold history of Climb Canyon and claim your event track rewards!

🧥 Exclusive 10th Anniversary skins  
🤑 Special Anniversary Month Offers  
🛠️ Under-the-hood bug fixes and improvements
